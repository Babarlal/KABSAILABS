4. The live voice demo

The demo at /demo uses Retell AI to run a real-time voice conversation. When you press “Start Call” and grant microphone permission, your audio is streamed to Retell, which converts it to text, generates a response, and returns synthesised speech. A transcript is displayed in your browser during the call.

We use this only to demonstrate the product. We do not use demo audio or transcripts for marketing, and we do not attempt to identify you from them. Retell processes this data as our sub-processor under their own terms and retention policy.

If you would rather not have your voice processed, simply don't start the demo. Nothing else on the site requires microphone access.

5. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-EMPMVS2NYX) to understand how the site is used. Analytics cookies are set only after you press “Accept” on the cookie banner. Until then, Google Consent Mode holds analytics storage in a denied state and no analytics cookies are written.

Pressing “Reject” means no analytics cookies are set at all. Your choice is stored locally in your browser. To change it, clear this site's data in your browser settings and the banner will reappear.

We also use Vercel Speed Insights, which measures page-load performance. It does not use cookies and does not track you across sites.
