Things people ask on the call

Does Shopify Flow already do this?

For one store, mostly yes, and we will tell you so. Flow cannot see Amazon, eBay, Etsy or your 3PL. That gap is the whole job.

I already have an inventory app.

Some are good. If yours updates every channel in under a minute and copes with returns and partial shipments, keep it. The audit is where we check, and we would rather say keep it than sell you a second one.

What about my email and SMS flows?

Not our job. Klaviyo owns that and does it properly. We build the stock side and leave your marketing alone.

How is it priced?

A fixed price for the build and a monthly fee for keeping it running. Never per order, never per ticket. The quote comes after the audit, once we have seen your setup, because a two channel store and a six channel store are not the same build.

How long does a build take?

Focused builds ship in 4 to 6 weeks. Larger multi system builds run 2 to 4 months, delivered in 1 to 2 week increments so you see progress early.

What breaks, and who fixes it?

APIs change. When one does, the sync pauses on that channel, a person gets the alert, and it is usually fixed before you notice. You get a message either way. That is the difference between a file and a system.
