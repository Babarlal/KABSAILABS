✦ How it works

From ringing phone to job on your screen.

Five things happen on every call, whether you're on a job, asleep, or on a ladder.

01

It picks up

First ring, any hour. No menu, no "press 1" — the caller just talks like they would to a person.

02

Name, address, what's broken, how bad. The questions you'd ask if you could take the call yourself.

The questions plumbers actually ask.

Does it sound like a robot?+
No. It's a natural voice and a real back-and-forth conversation — the caller asks something, it answers, it asks the next question. Most people don't clock it. Book the call and you can ring it yourself before you decide anything.
Do I have to change my phone number?+
No. It sits behind the number you already advertise. You choose when it answers — only after hours, only when you don't pick up within a few rings, or every call. Your choice.
What if it can't answer something?+
It's built to say it'll have someone call you back rather than guess. It won't invent a price or promise a time slot it can't keep — that's deliberate, because a wrong quote costs you more than a missed one.
Can it quote a price?+
It can give the starting prices you tell it to give, exactly as you've written them. It won't guarantee a final number over the phone — that stays with you, on site.
Where do the jobs go?+
Wherever you already work. A spreadsheet, your CRM, a text to your phone, or all three. If you don't run anything yet, we'll set up something simple.
How long until it's live?+
About a week for a standard setup — we write it around your services, your area, and your prices, then you test it before it takes a single real call.
