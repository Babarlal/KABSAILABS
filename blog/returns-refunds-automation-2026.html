Returns That Handle Themselves

Returns eat teams alive after every sale season. How to automate the routine ones and only look at the odd ones.

“Returns eat my team alive after every sale season.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

A return request gets checked against your policy, approved or declined, issued a label, and the customer kept informed. All without a person.

Anything unusual, expensive or borderline gets sent to a human with the details already gathered.

How it works

  1. The request comes in anywhere

    Email, chat, a form, your website.

  2. It is checked against your rules

    Within the window, in the right condition, eligible category.

  3. Straightforward ones complete themselves

    Label issued, customer updated, refund queued. Odd ones escalated.
