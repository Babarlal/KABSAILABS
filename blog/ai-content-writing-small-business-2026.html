“I know I should be posting but I never do.”

What it actually is

Blog posts, emails and social copy written in your voice, on a schedule, from the topics that matter to your customers.

Be clear about what this is: it produces good drafts, not finished work. Someone still reads it before it goes out. That someone just does five minutes instead of two hours.

How it works

  1. It learns how you sound

    From what you have already written and how you talk to customers.

  2. It writes on a schedule

    Topics from your services, your questions, your seasons.

  3. You approve before anything publishes

    Nothing goes out unread. That is the point.
