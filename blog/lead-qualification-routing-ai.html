What it actually is

Every enquiry gets asked the questions that separate a real job from a browse. Budget, timeline, location, what they actually need.

Then it goes to the right person. Big jobs to you, small ones to the team, out-of-area ones politely declined without you ever seeing them.

How it works

  1. It asks the qualifying questions

    The same ones you would ask, asked immediately instead of three days later.

  2. It scores what comes back

    Serious enquiries get flagged. Tyre kickers get a polite answer and no more of your time.

  3. It routes to the right person

    By value, by area, by service, by whoever is free. Whatever rule you want.
