WhatsApp and SMS Support: Meeting Customers Where They Already Are

Most customers would rather text than email or call. Here is how to answer them properly without living on your phone.

“Customers message us at midnight and expect an answer.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

The same knowledgeable assistant as website chat, but on WhatsApp or plain text message, where most of your customers actually prefer to talk.

It handles the ordinary questions any hour of the day and pulls a human in the moment something needs one.

How it works

  1. It sits on your business number

    Customers message the number they already have.

  2. It answers properly, day or night

    Order status, opening hours, availability, prices, next steps.

  3. Real people take over when it matters

    Complaints, complicated requests and anything expensive go straight to your team with the full thread.
