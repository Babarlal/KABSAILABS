A Website Chat That Actually Knows Your Business

Not the useless chat bubble from 2019. A chat that knows your prices, stock and policies, and hands to a human when it should.

“My team answers the same ten questions all day long.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

A chat window on your site that has read everything you know. Your prices, your delivery times, your returns policy, your opening hours, your product details.

When someone asks something it cannot answer, it stops guessing and passes the conversation to a person, with the whole history attached.

How it works

  1. You hand over what you know

    Website, price list, policies, a spreadsheet of common questions. Whatever exists.

  2. It answers in your voice

    Same tone, same rules. If you never discount, it never discounts.

  3. It knows when to stop

    Anything unusual, angry or expensive goes to a human immediately.
