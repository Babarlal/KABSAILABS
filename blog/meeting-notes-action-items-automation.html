The Meeting Notes Nobody Ever Writes

Decisions made on calls, captured and turned into follow-ups that actually get sent. Here is how it works.

“We agree things on calls and then nobody does them.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

Every call becomes a short summary, a list of decisions, and a set of actions with names against them. The follow-up email drafts itself.

Nothing depends on whoever remembered to take notes.

How it works

  1. It joins or takes the recording

    Whatever you use for calls.

  2. It writes what matters

    Decisions and actions. Not a transcript nobody reads.

  3. The follow-up is ready to send

    Drafted, waiting for you to glance at it and press send.
