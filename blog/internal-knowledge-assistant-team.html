Stop Your Best People Being the Company Search Engine

When your senior staff spend the day answering the team's questions, nothing else gets done. Here is the fix.

“My best people spend all day answering the team.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

An assistant that has read your internal documents. Processes, pricing rules, supplier terms, product specs, how you do things.

Your team asks it instead of interrupting whoever knows. It answers from your actual documents and says so, rather than making something up.

How it works

  1. It reads what you already have

    Handbooks, shared drives, past decisions, spec sheets.

  2. Your team asks in plain language

    Wherever they already work. Usually a chat tool.

  3. It shows its working

    Every answer points at the document it came from, so nobody has to take its word.
