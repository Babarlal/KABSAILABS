Chasing the Leads Who Went Quiet

Most enquiries never say no. They just go silent. Here is how to follow up properly without nagging or forgetting.

“Half my leads just vanish and I never follow up.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

Anyone who enquired and went quiet gets a sequence of genuinely useful messages over the following weeks. Not nagging. Answers to the objections they never voiced.

It stops the moment they reply or book, and hands straight to you.

How it works

  1. Silence starts the sequence

    No quote goes unchased ever again.

  2. Each message earns its place

    A reassurance, a case study, a question, a straightforward offer. Spread over weeks, not days.

  3. It stops the second they engage

    The moment they reply, the automation gets out of the way.
