Booking Appointments Without the Back and Forth

Four messages to agree a time is three too many. How automatic booking takes an enquiry straight to a slot in the diary.

What it actually is

The enquiry goes all the way to a confirmed slot in your calendar without a person touching it.

It knows your real availability, your travel time between jobs, which staff do what, and how long each job takes.

How it works

  1. It checks the real diary

    Live availability, not a form that double books you.

  2. It offers times that actually work

    Accounting for travel, staff skills and job length.

  3. It confirms and reminds

    Confirmation immediately, reminder before, reschedule link if they need it.
