Doing the Outreach Nobody Has Time For

Finding prospects, researching them and reaching out personally, at a volume no person could manage.

“Nobody here has time to prospect consistently.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

It builds a list of businesses that match your best customers, researches each one properly, writes outreach that refers to something true about them, and follows up.

Replies go to a person. The grinding part, which is why outreach always stops, is the part that gets automated.

How it works

  1. It builds the list

    Businesses that look like the ones you already serve well.

  2. It researches each one

    Something real and specific about that business, not a merged first name.

  3. It reaches out and follows up

    Then hands every reply to a human immediately.
