Stop Typing Numbers Off Paperwork

Invoices, receipts, forms and contracts, read automatically and put where they belong. What it does and what it saves.

“Someone here types these into a spreadsheet all week.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

Documents get read and the important numbers pulled out. Invoices, receipts, delivery notes, application forms, contracts.

The information lands in your spreadsheet or accounting system without anyone typing it. Anything it is unsure about gets flagged rather than guessed.

How it works

  1. Documents arrive however they already arrive

    Email attachment, scan, photo, shared folder.

  2. The numbers get pulled out

    Amounts, dates, reference numbers, supplier names, line items.

  3. It lands where you need it

    Your sheet, your accounting software, your system. Odd ones flagged for a human.
