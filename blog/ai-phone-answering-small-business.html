AI That Answers Your Phone: The 2026 Guide for Busy Trades and Clinics

What an AI receptionist actually does, what it costs, and how to tell if your business is losing money to unanswered calls.

“Every call I miss is a job someone else got.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

An AI voice picks up the calls you cannot. After hours, on a job, on another line, or at nine on a Sunday night.

It talks like a person. It asks what the caller needs, answers the questions your website already answers, takes the details, and books the work into your calendar. Anything it cannot handle, it flags to you straight away with a summary.

How it works

  1. Your number forwards when nobody picks up

    Nothing changes about the number you advertise. Calls only reach the AI when they would otherwise have hit voicemail.

  2. It has a conversation

    It knows your services, your prices, your area, your hours. It asks the questions your receptionist would ask.

  3. You get a job sheet, not a voicemail

    Name, number, address, what the problem is, how urgent it sounds. In your phone before the caller has put theirs down.
