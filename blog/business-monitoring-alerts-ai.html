Finding Out Before Your Customer Does

Stock, prices, uptime, reviews, competitors. How automatic monitoring tells you the moment something changes.

“We find out something broke when a customer tells us.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

Something that matters gets watched constantly. Stock levels, competitor prices, whether your site is up, new reviews, orders stuck in transit.

When it changes in a way you care about, you are told immediately, through whatever you actually check.

How it works

  1. You choose what matters

    The thing that costs you money when it goes wrong unnoticed.

  2. It watches continuously

    Every few minutes, all day, without getting bored.

  3. You hear about it fast

    Text, email or chat. Only when it crosses the line you set.
