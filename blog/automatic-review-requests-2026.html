Getting Reviews Without Asking Awkwardly

Reviews decide who gets called first. How to ask every happy customer automatically and handle unhappy ones privately.

“My competitor has 400 reviews and I have 30.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

Every completed job triggers a review request at the moment goodwill is highest. Usually a text, because that is what people actually open.

If someone is unhappy, it catches that first and routes them to you privately instead of onto a public page.

How it works

  1. Finishing the job sets it off

    From your calendar, your invoice, or a tap on your phone.

  2. It asks at the right moment

    Same day, while they still remember you fondly.

  3. Unhappy customers reach you first

    A quiet question filters them to you before they post.
