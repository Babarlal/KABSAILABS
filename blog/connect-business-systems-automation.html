Making Your Systems Talk to Each Other

If your team types the same thing into three places, this is the fix. How system integration works in plain English.

“We type the same thing into three different systems.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

Your customer system, your accounting software, your operations sheet and your inbox all get told the same thing at the same time, in both directions.

Enter something once. Everywhere else updates itself.

How it works

  1. We map what lives where

    Usually the first time anyone has written it down.

  2. Information flows both ways

    A change in one place updates the others, not just one direction.

  3. Disagreements get flagged

    When two systems conflict, someone is told instead of it silently rotting.
