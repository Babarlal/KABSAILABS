What it actually is

Ask for a company and get back a briefing. What they do, who runs it, how big they are, what has changed lately, what it might mean for you.

It gathers and organises. You still bring the judgement, but you start from a full picture instead of a blank page.

How it works

  1. You name the company or market

    One line is enough.

  2. It gathers from public sources

    Website, filings, news, job postings, social.

  3. You get a briefing, not a link dump

    Organised the way you would want it, with sources shown.
