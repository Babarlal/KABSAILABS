Waking Up the Customers Who Stopped Buying

Your old customer list is the cheapest sales channel you own. How automatic win-back campaigns bring them back.

“I have a database full of old customers doing nothing.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

It finds everyone who used to buy and stopped, works out roughly when they went quiet, and runs a return offer at them.

These people already know you and already trusted you once. They are far cheaper to win back than a stranger is to win at all.

How it works

  1. It works out who has gone quiet

    Based on how often they used to buy, not a blanket rule.

  2. It sends something worth opening

    A reason to come back, not a guilt trip.

  3. Anyone who bites reaches a human

    Warm replies go to your team, not into a funnel.
