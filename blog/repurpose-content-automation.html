Turning One Video Into a Month of Posts

You make good things once and waste them. How automatic repurposing turns a single piece into content everywhere.

“We make good stuff once and then waste it.”

Said by a business owner, more or less word for word

What it actually is

One podcast, video, webinar or long article becomes clips, quotes, threads, a newsletter and a week of posts, each shaped for the platform it lands on.

The thinking was already done. This just stops it being used once and forgotten.

How it works

  1. You publish one substantial thing

    A video, an episode, a talk, a long post.

  2. It gets broken into pieces

    The strong moments, the quotable lines, the useful bits.

  3. Each piece is reshaped per platform

    What works on LinkedIn is not what works on Instagram.
