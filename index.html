Common questions

What does KABS AI LABS actually do?+
We build custom AI agents, workflow automation and analytics dashboards, wired into the tools you already use. For eCommerce brands, that usually starts with keeping stock, orders, refunds and payouts in sync across channels.
How long does a project take?+
Focused builds ship in 4 to 6 weeks. Larger multi system builds run 2 to 4 months, delivered in 1 to 2 week increments so you see progress early.
Do we have to switch our tools?+
No. We integrate with what you run: Shopify, Amazon, HubSpot, Stripe, Slack, GoHighLevel, GA4, and most platforms with an API. If one of your tools is the actual problem, we'll say so, and the choice stays yours.
How do we start?+
Book a free 30 minute audit. You walk us through where the copying and checking happens, we map it while you talk, and we put rough hours next to each gap using your numbers. You leave with the three fixes worth doing first. Nothing is committed until you approve a fixed price quote.
Which industries does KABS AI LABS work with?+
eCommerce brands first, then marketing agencies, real estate, SaaS, healthcare and service businesses. That includes white label automation for agencies reselling under their own brand.
What results can we expect from AI automation?+
It depends on the gap, which is why we do the arithmetic before we quote. On the audit we put hours next to each manual step using your numbers, so you can see whether a build pays for itself before you decide. If it doesn't, we'll tell you.
Will it break something?+
We build alongside your live systems, test against real data in read only mode first, and switch on one gap at a time. You approve each step.
What happens when Shopify or Amazon changes something?+
That’s the part most people forget to ask. We stay on the build after launch. When something upstream changes, fixing it is our job.
