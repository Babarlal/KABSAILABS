What the price covers

The build is fixed

You get the figure before work starts, and it does not move because the job took longer than we thought. If the audit finds the work is smaller than it looked, the quote comes down.

The retainer is for keeping it right

Models drift, tools change their APIs, and your business changes what it asks. The monthly fee covers watching for that, fixing it, and a report on what ran.

There is no usage meter

No per-message, per-minute or per-ticket billing. A busy month costs what a quiet one does, so nobody has to ration the thing you just paid to build.

The audit comes first, free

Bring a week of real examples. We come back with the three worth doing first, what each is worth, and a fixed price. If a cheaper tool already covers it, we say so.
