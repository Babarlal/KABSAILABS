↗ What a workflow actually does

One event in. It moves the data itself.

No magic and no brittle spreadsheet macro. An event fires in one tool, the workflow reads it, does the work across your other tools, and flags a person only when something does not match. Here is the loop it runs every time.

01 · Trigger

An event fires

A new order, a paid invoice, a form submission, a row added. The workflow starts the moment it happens, not on a schedule.

02 · Move

Carry the data across

Reads the record from one tool and writes it into the next in the right shape. No window-switching, no re-keying.

03 · Act

Do the next step

Updates stock, posts to accounting, sends the confirmation, files the report. The action, done end to end.

04 · Flag

Catch the exceptions

A mismatch, a missing field, a failed step goes to a person with the full context, so nothing fails silently.

The same copy-paste, look-up and update, done a few hundred times a week, is what a workflow takes off your team. The odd exception that needs judgement is the only thing a person still touches — that split is the whole design.
