Questions people ask on the call

What can you automate for an online store?

The gaps between your systems: inventory sync and oversell prevention, order and payout reconciliation, order status support on chat, WhatsApp and phone, wholesale ordering, returns and refunds, and supplier invoices and POs. We don’t do marketing automation.

Will it handle peak season?

That’s what it’s for. Volume goes up, the copying and checking doesn’t. The one thing we set up before peak season is buffers per channel, so a fast selling Amazon listing never pulls the floor out from under your own store.

We already use an inventory app. Why would we need you?

Keep it. Apps handle the common case well. The gaps are the exceptions: bundles, backorders, a 3PL feed that arrives late, a channel the app doesn’t cover. We build for the exceptions, on top of what you run. Quick check: since you got the app, has it been zero oversells, or just fewer?

How long does it take?

Inventory sync between two channels is faster than a full wholesale portal. You get a written timeline after the Gap Review, before you commit to anything. Focused builds usually ship in 3 to 5 weeks.

Is this for us?

It’s a fit if you sell on Shopify plus at least one more channel and ship enough that a mistake costs real money. It’s not a fit for dropshipping stores or single channel shops. We’ll say so on the call and point you somewhere useful.

What happens when Amazon or Shopify changes something?

We stay on the build after launch. When something upstream changes, fixing it is our job.

Does Shopify Flow already do this?

For one store, mostly yes, and we will tell you so. Flow cannot see Amazon, eBay, Etsy or your 3PL. That gap is the whole job.

What about my email and SMS flows?

Not our job. Klaviyo owns that and does it properly. We build the stock side and leave your marketing alone.

How is it priced?

A fixed price for the build and a monthly fee for keeping it running. Never per order, never per ticket. The quote comes after the audit, once we have seen your setup, because a two channel store and a six channel store are not the same build.

What breaks, and who fixes it?

APIs change. When one does, the sync pauses on that channel, a person gets the alert, and it is usually fixed before you notice. You get a message either way. That is the difference between a file and a system.
