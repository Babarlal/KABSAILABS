FAQ

What founders ask first.

We use Stripe's built-in retries. Isn't that enough?+
It's a start. Default retries recover roughly a quarter to a third of failed charges. Smart timing, a pre-expiry card nudge and a short email sequence take that to 70% or more. The gap is revenue you already earned.
Does it work with our billing and CRM?+
Stripe, Chargebee, Paddle, and HubSpot, GoHighLevel or Salesforce on the CRM side. We wire into what you run; nothing gets replaced.
Will it spam our customers?+
No. Retries are silent first; a message only goes out when a retry can't fix it, and it says exactly what to do. Fewer, better touches convert better and annoy less.
How is it priced for a startup?+
A fixed build fee, then a monthly fee to keep it running. Never a cut of recovered revenue, so what we win back is yours.
