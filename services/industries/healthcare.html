Yes. We design intake and call flows around your privacy and compliance requirements and involve your compliance team early. We don't provide legal advice; your counsel signs off.
Will it replace front-desk staff?+
No. It takes repetitive and after-hours calls off their plate so they can focus on patients in front of them.
Can it book into our system?+
Yes, it books into your scheduler and can hand intake details to your EHR/CRM.
