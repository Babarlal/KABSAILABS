FAQ

Voice AI Agents FAQ

Will it sound like a robot?+
No — we use best-in-class voices (e.g. ElevenLabs) for natural, back-and-forth conversation, not a phone tree.
Can it use our existing number?+
Yes — we route your current line (via Twilio/telephony) so callers reach the agent with no change on their end.
What happens on complex calls?+
The agent qualifies and books, and transfers to a human the moment a call needs one — with the context passed along.
How fast can it go live?+
A focused inbound agent can be live in 2-4 weeks, including CRM booking and call transcripts.
