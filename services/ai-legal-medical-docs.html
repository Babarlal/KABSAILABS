AI for Legal & Medical Docs FAQ

Is this built with privacy in mind?+
Yes. We design around your privacy and compliance requirements and involve your compliance/legal team early. We don't provide legal advice. Your counsel signs off.
Can it cite its sources?+
Every answer links back to the exact document and page it came from, so your team can verify before relying on it.
What about sensitive data?+
We support access controls, redaction, and self-hosted/region-locked deployments depending on your requirements.
How long to deploy?+
A focused pilot over one document set typically ships in 4–6 weeks.
