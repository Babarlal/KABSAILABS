FAQ

AI Automation Agency FAQ

What kinds of AI agents can you build?+
Chat and voice agents that qualify leads, answer questions, schedule, follow up, and take actions in your tools — grounded in your own data and policies.
Will an agent make mistakes or "hallucinate"?+
We ground agents in your data, add guardrails and evaluations, and route anything uncertain to a human, so answers stay accurate and safe.
Which tools and models do you use?+
Tool-agnostic — OpenAI, Claude, or Gemini for reasoning; n8n, Make, or Zapier for orchestration; wired into your existing CRM and comms stack.
How long until an agent is live?+
Focused agents ship in 4-6 weeks; larger multi-agent systems run 2-4 months in 1-2 week increments.
