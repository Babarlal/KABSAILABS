↗ What an agent actually does

One message in. Four steps, every time.

Not magic, and not a chatbot that guesses. An agent reads the request, finds the answer in your own tools, takes the action, and knows when to pull in a person. Here is the loop it runs on every message.

01 · Read

Understand the ask

Parses the email, chat or call and works out what is actually wanted, in your language and your product's terms.

02 · Retrieve

Look it up in your data

Checks the order, the CRM record, the policy doc. Grounded in your systems, so the answer is real, not invented.

03 · Act

Do the next step

Replies, books the slot, updates the record, raises the ticket. The action, not just the answer.

04 · Escalate

Know when to stop

Anything unsure or unusual goes to a person, with the whole thread attached. It never guesses on the ones that matter.

Roughly the same twenty questions and five decisions, made a few hundred times a week, are what an agent takes off your team. The rare, judgement-heavy 10% stays with a human — that split is the whole design.
