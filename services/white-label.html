FAQ

What partners ask first.

Will my client ever know KABS exists?+
No. We work under NDA, in your accounts, and never contact your client unless you introduce us. Every artefact carries your brand, not ours.
How is partner pricing different?+
A fixed build fee per project and a monthly fee to keep it running, discounted on volume so your margin is predictable. You set the retail price to your client.
Who handles support?+
We monitor and fix in the background; you stay the face. When something needs the client, it comes from you, on our brief.
Can we start with one project?+
Yes. Most partners test with a single client build, then roll it out as a productised line once they've seen it land.
