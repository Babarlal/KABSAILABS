↗ How RAG actually answers

A question in. A sourced answer out.

Not a chatbot guessing from the open internet. It searches your own documents, pulls the passages that match, answers from those alone, and shows you the page it used. If the answer is not in your files, it says so instead of inventing one.

01 · Ask

A question comes in

From chat, email or a call. The system reads what is actually being asked, in your product's own terms.

02 · Retrieve

Search your documents

Finds the exact passages that match across your manuals, policies, contracts and past tickets. Your files, not the web.

03 · Answer

Reply, with the citation

Writes the answer grounded in those passages and shows the source page, so anyone can check where it came from.

04 · Defer

Know what it doesn't know

If the documents don't cover it, it says so and hands to a person, instead of guessing. That restraint is the point.

The same questions your team answers from the same handful of documents, a few hundred times a week, are what RAG takes off them. The genuinely new questions still reach a person — grounded answers plus honest "I don't know" is the whole design.
