Line-by-line payroll and invoice validation with an exception queue

Every row is checked against seven rules. Clean rows and exceptions go to separate queues, a summary goes to Slack and Gmail, and every decision is logged.

The problem

Payroll and invoice data goes wrong at the line, not the total. Negative hours, a duplicated shift, tips far out of line with sales, a rate that does not match the role, a shift outside the pay period. Each can pass a totals check and still be wrong. Reviewing every line by hand is slow, so errors reach the pay run.

How it works

  1. Normalize: trim, convert numbers, and stamp each row with its line number.
  2. Validate each row against the checks below.
  3. Route clean rows to the validated output and failures to the exception queue.
  4. Post a summary to Slack and Gmail and write every row to an audit log.

The checks

  • Employee ID present.
  • Date inside the pay period.
  • Hours between 0 and 16.
  • Rate matches the role table.
  • Tips no more than 40% of sales.
  • No duplicate employee, date and pay component.
  • No employee with two roles in one shift.

Verified run

A live run checked 30 rows. 22 passed and 8 were exceptions, and each planted error tripped exactly one rule. The exception queue shows the line number, the employee, the rule in plain English and the raw values, for example hours logged as negative 5, or $250 in tips on $90 of sales.

What a person still handles

  • Reviewing and correcting each exception.
  • Approving the pay run.
