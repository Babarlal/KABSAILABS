The Challenge

Weekly reporting meant stitching together exports from the store, ad platforms, and email by hand: a full day of copy-paste every week, and numbers nobody fully trusted.

The Solution

We built one pipeline that pulls every source into a warehouse automatically and a live dashboard on top, so reporting is always current.

How We Built It

Scheduled n8n workflows extract from each source, clean and de-duplicate the records, land them in BigQuery, and feed a Looker Studio dashboard.

  • Automated extraction from store, ads, and email platforms
  • Cleaning and de-duplication before warehousing
  • Central BigQuery warehouse for fast queries
  • Live Looker Studio dashboard with daily and weekly roll-ups

Results

Reporting went from a full day each week to near real-time, about 90% faster, and the team finally works from one trusted set of numbers.

Key Features

  • Automated multi-source ETL
  • Unified data warehouse
  • Live, always-current dashboards
  • Automated daily and weekly roll-ups

Technical Stack

n8n, Google BigQuery, Looker Studio, Shopify, and GA4, connected via APIs.
