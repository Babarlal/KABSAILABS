The Challenge

New signups often stalled before reaching the product’s aha moment. Manual onboarding emails were generic, mistimed, and didn’t scale with signups.

The Solution

We built event-driven lifecycle automation that reacts to what each user actually does in the product and sends the most relevant nudge next.

How We Built It

Product events stream into a workflow that branches on user behavior, picks the next best message, and tracks progress toward activation.

  • Listens to real product events, not just time delays
  • Branches sequences based on what the user has and hasn’t done
  • Sends in-app and email nudges at the right moment
  • Tracks each user’s progress to the activation milestone

Results

Activation rose 31%, manual outreach dropped sharply, and the team gained clear visibility into where users get stuck in the funnel.

Key Features

  • Event-driven triggers
  • Behavior-based branching
  • In-app + email nudges
  • Activation funnel tracking

Technical Stack

Event tracking, n8n, HubSpot, PostgreSQL, and OpenAI, connected via APIs.
