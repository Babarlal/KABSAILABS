Meeting follow-ups that turn notes into tasks and contact updates

When a meeting ends, the action items go into ClickUp as assigned tasks and the notes go to the contact's record in ActiveCampaign.

The problem

The team held many meetings a week: internal check-ins, project discussions and client calls. After each one someone rewatched the recording or read the Fathom notes, copied action items into ClickUp, assigned them, wrote a summary email for external contacts and checked nothing was missed. That took 20 to 30 minutes per meeting, and action items still slipped and client updates arrived days late.

How it works

  1. Fathom identifies the key notes and action items.
  2. The items go to ClickUp, where tasks are created and assigned to the right people.
  3. The notes are added to the contact's record in ActiveCampaign. If the person is not in the system yet, a new contact is created.

It runs in the background after every meeting. What took 20 to 30 minutes now happens in seconds.

What a person still handles

  • Doing the tasks.
  • Checking the notes Fathom picked up.
